🪰 What's real / what's scaffold

A real full-connectome simulation decides when and how; a scripted mission decides what. Same honest deal as every viral connectome demo — here's the exact split.

✅ Real

  • All 139,255 neurons and ~2.7M synaptic connections of the FlyWire FAFB v783 connectome are simulated server-side, 10 ticks/sec — the full anatomical wiring diagram, not a subset or a recording.
  • The causal chain: food odor stimulates the actual OLF_ORN_FOOD/GUS_GRN_SWEET neuron populations → spikes cascade through the real connectome → feeding-circuit activity drives the feed behavior. Zero the connectome and nothing deploys.
  • The walking, searching, grooming detours, and the startle after a failed deploy (a nociception stimulus into the real NOCI population) are all live simulation output — every run is different.
  • The Railway deployments are real GraphQL mutations, spending real money — verify the services yourself on the project's public dashboard.
  • The spike raster below the board shows live per-group firing from the sim, streamed at 10Hz.

🔧 Scaffold

  • The neuron dynamics are simple leaky integrate-and-fire with uniform parameters — real anatomy, toy physiology. Nobody has biophysically calibrated parameters for 139K neurons.
  • FlyWire covers the brain only: there is no ventral nerve cord in the dataset, so motor neuron outputs are synthesized from real descending-neuron activity by hand-written code. Real brain, puppet spinal cord.
  • Behavior selection is a thresholded readout state machine over neuron-group activity, and drives (hunger, fear…) are scalar variables injected as stimulation — scaffolding around the sim, not emergent from it.
  • The mission stacks the deck: while a step is pending, hunger is clamped high and the food has a board-wide odor plume. The fly was always going to want the food — but finding, reaching, and eating it is its own doing.
  • No learning: synaptic weights never change. Run 500 is statistically identical to run 1 — the best-time board measures luck, not improvement.
